**Quick answer:** Veterans’ Group Life Insurance policyholders with active coverage on November 1, 2026, generally receive a premium holiday through January 31, 2027. VA applies it automatically and coverage continues under existing terms. Your first premium and old balances still matter. New or reinstated coverage may qualify for fewer months.

## What the premium holiday changes

Veterans’ Group Life Insurance, or VGLI, is group life insurance available to eligible people after military service. A premium is the amount you pay to keep insurance in force. During VA’s three-month premium holiday, eligible VGLI policyholders get a break on premiums for November and December 2026 and January 2027. The benefit amount and terms of coverage continue to apply.

Check the name of your coverage on your statement. VA says this break applies to VGLI. The rules for programs such as Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance and Veterans Affairs Life Insurance are separate. [VA’s VGLI Premium Holiday FAQ](https://benefits.va.gov/INSURANCE/vgli_premium_holiday_faqs.asp)

## Which months are covered?

Check the effective date on your coverage confirmation. That is the date coverage begins. For a policy that was already active going into November, the break generally applies to all three months. VA gives the following examples for people who enroll or reinstate around the start of the holiday.

| When VGLI takes effect | How the holiday works |
| --- | --- |
| Before November 1, 2026, and active on November 1 | November, December, and January premiums covered |
| During November 2026 | Pay the required first premium. December and January may be covered. |
| During December 2026 | Pay the required first premium. January may be covered. |
| January 1, 2027, or later | No months of the premium holiday apply. |

If your coverage ended on or before October 31, check any later reinstatement date with the Office of Servicemembers’ Group Life Insurance. Your first payment is required to activate new or reinstated coverage. [VA’s eligibility examples](https://benefits.va.gov/INSURANCE/vgli_premium_holiday_faqs.asp)

## Check how you pay

**VGLI AutoPay.** VA says the payment system will collect amounts for months outside the holiday as needed. You can review the statement to see how the credit was applied.

**Recurring payments set up through your bank.** A scheduled bank payment is different from VGLI AutoPay. Check with your bank about adjusting the recurring transfer for eligible months and restarting regular payments in February. Before making changes, check whether your statement includes an older balance or any months outside the holiday.

**Military retirement or VA disability deductions.** VA says eligible deductions pause during the holiday and resume afterward. If you pay quarterly or annually and already paid for eligible months, VA says those payments appear as credits on a billing statement. [VA’s guidance by payment method](https://benefits.va.gov/INSURANCE/vgli_premium_holiday_faqs.asp)

## Read the bill by coverage month

You can still receive a statement during the holiday. A bill that comes in January can be for February coverage. If you pay for several months at once, some months on that statement may fall within the holiday and others after it. An overdue balance from before November remains due.

For example, say your annual billing statement comes in December and shows coverage through the following fall. Compare the months covered by the bill with the credit. If you already sent a payment for an eligible month, VA says it can show up as a credit against a future bill. The program administrator can explain your account balance and refund options. [VA billing and credit guidance](https://benefits.va.gov/INSURANCE/vgli_premium_holiday_faqs.asp)

## Check the new amount if you increased coverage

VA says an eligible increase that takes effect during the holiday can also be covered for eligible months. The higher regular premium comes due after the holiday. If you recently chose a higher benefit, find the new premium on your paperwork before updating the amount you set aside each month. [VA’s guidance on coverage changes](https://benefits.va.gov/INSURANCE/vgli_premium_holiday_faqs.asp)

## Put the regular premium back into your budget

Pull up your current coverage amount and the regular premium you expect after January. If you had an age-based premium increase during the holiday, use the higher amount when you check the February budget. It’s also a good moment to confirm your beneficiary, the person or organization you chose to receive the benefit.

If your existing policy has a different purpose, such as replacing household income, put funeral costs on a separate line. Compare that amount with the savings and benefits you expect to be available. If you want help reading what private coverage could add, you can [schedule a consultation](/book) with a licensed agent. Contact the VGLI administrator for questions about your government program account.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Do I need to apply for the VGLI premium holiday?**

VA applies the holiday automatically for eligible coverage. Check your statement and effective date to confirm which months apply to your account.

**Will my coverage stop if I don’t pay for a covered month?**

VA says coverage continues under its existing terms during the holiday. Other amounts owed and regular premiums outside the holiday still apply.

**Does the holiday erase what I owed before November?**

Earlier balances still have to be paid. If you are unsure whether a statement reflects an old balance or an upcoming premium, check the coverage dates and contact the program administrator.
