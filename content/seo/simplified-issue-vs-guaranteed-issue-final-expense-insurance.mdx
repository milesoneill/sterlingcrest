**Quick answer:** Simplified issue policies ask health questions and offer lower premiums with faster benefit access. Guaranteed issue policies skip health screening, accept everyone, and charge higher premiums with a typical 2–3 year waiting period before full benefits pay.

## Which Final Expense Policy Type Fits Your Health and Budget?

Final expense insurance comes in two forms: simplified issue and guaranteed issue. Both cover funeral, burial, and end-of-life costs. They differ in how you qualify and what you pay.

Simplified issue asks health questions. Answer favorably, and you typically pay less with faster benefit access. Guaranteed issue skips all health screening. Everyone gets accepted. You pay higher premiums and wait 2–3 years for full benefits.

Your health history determines which type makes sense. If you can answer a few questions about recent diagnoses and hospitalizations, simplified issue usually costs less. If serious conditions would disqualify you elsewhere, guaranteed issue guarantees coverage.

## How Simplified Issue Works

Simplified issue skips the medical exam but asks health questions during your application. Expect questions about recent hospitalizations, current medications, chronic conditions, and major diagnoses in the past few years.

Meet the carrier's health guidelines and you're approved. Most policies pay the full death benefit immediately or after a short waiting period. Premiums run lower than guaranteed issue because the carrier screens out higher-risk applicants.

This works if you manage stable conditions. High blood pressure controlled with medication? Diabetes with stable blood sugar? Prior heart disease with no recent events? You may qualify. As long as your answers fit the carrier's rules, you get coverage at a better rate.

## How Guaranteed Issue Works

Guaranteed issue requires no medical exam and no health questions. Acceptance is automatic regardless of your health history.

Because carriers accept everyone blind, most policies include a graded waiting period of two to three years. Die from natural causes during that window and your beneficiaries receive premiums paid plus interest, not the full benefit. After the waiting period, full benefits pay out.

Premiums cost more than simplified issue for the same coverage. The carrier prices in the risk of insuring applicants without health information. This type exists for people who can't answer health questions favorably or who've been declined elsewhere.

## The Core Differences

**Health Questions**

Simplified issue asks them. Your answers determine approval. Guaranteed issue asks nothing and accepts everyone.

**Approval**

Simplified issue depends on your health meeting carrier guidelines. Guaranteed issue approves automatically.

**Waiting Periods**

Simplified issue often pays full benefits immediately or after a short wait. Guaranteed issue typically includes a 2–3 year graded period where early natural death returns premiums plus interest, not the full benefit.

**Cost**

Simplified issue costs less because health screening lets carriers assess risk. Guaranteed issue runs 30–50% higher for the same coverage. Carriers price in the unknown risk of accepting all applicants.

**Who It Serves**

Simplified issue fits people with manageable conditions who want lower premiums. Guaranteed issue serves people with serious health issues who need acceptance and will pay more for certainty.

## Understanding Waiting Periods

Most simplified issue policies pay full benefits from day one. Some carriers add a 12–24 month waiting period, but many offer immediate coverage if your health answers qualify you.

Guaranteed issue typically includes a two- to three-year graded period. Die from natural causes during that time and the policy returns premiums paid plus 10% interest. Your family gets the returned premiums, not the death benefit. Accidental death during the waiting period usually triggers full benefit payout immediately.

Once the waiting period ends, guaranteed issue pays the full benefit regardless of cause. The graded period protects carriers from immediate claims on high-risk applicants, which is how they can accept everyone without screening.

## Premium Comparison

Simplified issue costs less than guaranteed issue for identical coverage. Health questions let carriers identify lower-risk applicants and price accordingly.

Guaranteed issue costs more because the carrier accepts all applicants without health data. Premiums must account for the possibility that every applicant has serious conditions.

A 70-year-old seeking $10,000 coverage might pay $80–$100 monthly for simplified issue. The same person could pay $120–$150 for guaranteed issue. Exact amounts vary by age, coverage, gender, state, and carrier. Guaranteed issue always costs more for equivalent coverage.

## When Simplified Issue Makes Sense

Choose simplified issue if you have stable health conditions and can answer questions without recent hospitalizations or major diagnoses that would disqualify you.

You're likely a good fit if you take medication for controlled high blood pressure, manage Type 2 diabetes with stable levels, have heart disease history but no recent cardiac events, or handle other common conditions without recent hospitalization or treatment changes.

This type also fits if you want lower premiums and faster benefit access. If your health qualifies you, simplified issue offers better value. You avoid the higher cost of guaranteed acceptance.

## When Guaranteed Issue Makes Sense

Choose guaranteed issue if serious or multiple health conditions would likely trigger a decline for simplified coverage. Recent cancer, ongoing dialysis, advanced COPD, recent stroke or heart attack often make guaranteed issue your only option.

This type also works if you've been declined elsewhere or simply can't answer health questions favorably. You prioritize guaranteed acceptance over cost. You accept higher premiums and a waiting period in exchange for certainty.

If you're in poor health and need coverage regardless of price, guaranteed issue ensures your family receives at least returned premiums during the waiting period and full benefits after.

## Switching from Guaranteed to Simplified Issue

If your health improves after buying guaranteed issue, you can apply for simplified issue. This isn't a policy conversion. It's a new application with health questions.

Qualify for simplified issue and the premium drops? Replace your guaranteed policy with the new coverage. You'll answer health questions again and get approved based on current health.
