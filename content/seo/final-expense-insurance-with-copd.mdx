**Quick answer:** You can get Final Expense Insurance with COPD. Mild or stable COPD may qualify you for simplified issue policies with immediate benefits. Advanced COPD with oxygen use or recent hospitalizations typically qualifies for guaranteed issue with a waiting period.

## Can I Get Final Expense Insurance If I Have COPD?

Yes. A COPD diagnosis won't disqualify you.

What matters is how COPD affects your daily life and health stability. Carriers evaluate severity, not just the diagnosis itself. Mild, well-controlled COPD may qualify you for simplified issue policies with immediate death benefits. Regular oxygen use or recent hospitalizations typically lead to guaranteed issue policies that accept all applicants.

COPD severity determines *which type* of policy you qualify for, not *whether* you can get coverage. Knowing where your health falls on the spectrum helps you choose the right application path and set realistic expectations.

## How COPD Severity Affects Your Policy Options

Carriers group COPD into three categories. Each leads to different policy options.

**Mild or stable COPD:** You manage symptoms with inhalers or oral medications. You don't use supplemental oxygen. You haven't been hospitalized for COPD in the past 12–24 months. You may qualify for simplified issue Final Expense Insurance with immediate death benefits and no medical exam.

**Moderate COPD:** You experience more frequent flare-ups. You use nebulizers or steroids. You've had occasional emergency visits without hospitalization. Some carriers may still offer simplified issue. Others will direct you toward guaranteed issue. Underwriting guidelines vary by carrier.

**Severe or advanced COPD:** You use supplemental oxygen daily. You've been hospitalized for COPD exacerbations in the past year. Your lung function significantly limits daily activities. Guaranteed issue Final Expense Insurance is typically your best option. These policies accept all applicants without health questions but include a two- to three-year graded death benefit period.

Oxygen use is one of the clearest indicators. Night-only oxygen may still qualify you for simplified issue with some carriers. All-day oxygen typically means guaranteed issue is the reliable path.

## Simplified Issue vs. Guaranteed Issue Policies

**Simplified issue policies** ask 5–10 health questions covering recent hospitalizations, oxygen use, terminal illness, and other serious conditions. If your answers fall within the carrier's guidelines, you're approved. Your full death benefit is available immediately. No medical exam required. The trade-off: advanced COPD may disqualify you.

**Guaranteed issue policies** ask no health questions. Approval is guaranteed regardless of COPD severity, oxygen dependence, or hospitalization history. The trade-off: a graded death benefit period, typically two to three years. If you pass away from natural causes during that period, beneficiaries receive premiums paid plus interest, not the full death benefit. After the waiting period, full benefits apply. Accidental death during the waiting period usually pays the full benefit immediately.

For many people with advanced COPD, guaranteed issue is the only realistic option. It still accomplishes the goal: your family won't be burdened with funeral and burial costs.

## What Underwriters Look for When You Have COPD

Simplified issue carriers ask specific health questions to assess COPD severity. Knowing what they're looking for helps you answer honestly and choose the right policy type.

Common questions:

- **Do you currently use supplemental oxygen?** If yes, how often: only at night, or throughout the day?
- **Have you been hospitalized for COPD, pneumonia, or respiratory failure in the past 12 or 24 months?** They want the date and reason.
- **What medications do you currently take for COPD?** Inhalers (rescue and maintenance), oral steroids, nebulizers, other respiratory medications.
- **Do you currently smoke, or have you quit?** If you quit, when? Smoking status significantly affects eligibility and premium rates.
- **Have you been diagnosed with emphysema, chronic bronchitis, or other lung conditions in addition to COPD?** Some carriers evaluate these separately.
- **Have you had pulmonary function tests?** Not all carriers ask, but some may request FEV1 or other lung capacity measurements if available.

Underwriters use these answers to determine whether your COPD is stable or progressing. Honest answers are critical. If you understate oxygen use or omit a recent hospitalization and a claim is filed within the first two years (the contestability period), the carrier can investigate and potentially deny the claim based on misrepresentation.

If health questions disqualify you from simplified issue, guaranteed issue is the appropriate policy type for your situation.

## Coverage Amounts and Costs With COPD

Premiums are based on age, gender, smoking status, coverage amount, and health. COPD affects your cost, but how much depends on severity and policy type.

If you qualify for simplified issue, expect higher premiums than someone without COPD, especially if you're a current or recent smoker. Carriers price simplified issue based on health question answers. COPD increases risk.

If you apply for guaranteed issue, premiums are standardized by age and coverage amount. Everyone in the same age bracket pays the same rate regardless of health. Guaranteed issue premiums are often higher than simplified issue premiums for healthy applicants but may be comparable or better for applicants with serious health conditions.

Typical coverage amounts range from $5,000 to $35,000. Most families choose $10,000 to $15,000 to cover funeral, burial, and immediate final expenses. ## Frequently Asked Questions

**Will I be denied if I use oxygen?**

Not necessarily. If you use oxygen throughout the day or have been hospitalized recently, you likely won't qualify for simplified issue, but you will still qualify for guaranteed issue, which accepts all applicants regardless of oxygen use. If you only use oxygen at night and your COPD is otherwise stable, some simplified issue carriers may still approve you.

**Does my smoking history affect my rates?**

Yes. Current smokers and recent quitters typically pay higher premiums than non-smokers, especially for simplified issue policies. If you quit smoking years ago, some carriers may offer non-smoker rates depending on how long it's been. Guaranteed issue policies are priced by age and coverage amount, so smoking status may have less impact on cost, but it's still a factor in some carrier pricing.

**Can I get immediate coverage with COPD?**

It depends on your COPD severity. If your COPD is mild or stable (no oxygen use and no recent hospitalizations), you may qualify for simplified issue with immediate death benefits. If your COPD is advanced, you'll likely qualify for guaranteed issue, which includes a two- to three-year waiting period before full benefits apply.

**What if I've been hospitalized for COPD recently?**

A recent hospitalization typically disqualifies you from simplified issue, but you will still qualify for guaranteed issue. Most simplified issue carriers ask whether you've been hospitalized in the past 12 to 24 months, and a yes answer usually results in a decline. Guaranteed issue policies don't ask health questions, so recent hospitalizations don't affect your eligibility.

**How do I know which policy type is right for me?**

The best way to know is to discuss your specific health situation with a licensed insurance professional who can compare your options across multiple carriers. If your COPD is mild and stable, simplified issue may be an option. If you use oxygen daily or have been hospitalized recently, guaranteed issue is the more reliable path.

**Can I get coverage if I have COPD and other health conditions?**

Yes. Many people with COPD also have diabetes, high blood pressure, or heart disease. Guaranteed issue accepts applicants regardless of how many health conditions they have. For simplified issue, eligibility depends on the combination of conditions and how each carrier underwrites them.
